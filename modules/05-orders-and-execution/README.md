# Module 05 — Orders and Execution

**Time:** ~4 hours · **Prerequisites:** Modules 01–04

Knowing what to trade is useless without knowing how to get in and out. This module
covers the order types, what they guarantee, what they do not, and the gap between the
price you see and the price you get.

## Lessons

1. [Order types](01-order-types.md) — market, limit, stop, and their entry variants
2. [Stops, targets and trailing stops](02-stops-and-targets.md) — exit orders in detail
3. [Slippage, gaps and rejections](03-execution-quality.md) — what goes wrong
4. [Managing a position](04-position-management.md) — from entry to exit

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this module you can:

- Choose the correct order type for a given intention and explain the trade-off
- Place a stop entry and a limit entry and say precisely how they differ
- Explain what a stop loss guarantees and what it does not
- Predict when slippage will be worst and in which direction it will cut
- Explain why a long position's stop triggers off the bid, and why spread widening alone
  can trigger it
- Describe when a trailing stop helps and when it converts winners into scratches
