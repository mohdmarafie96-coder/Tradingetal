# Module 05 Quiz

Closed book. [Answers](../../reference/quiz-answers.md#module-05).

**1.** State what a market order guarantees and what a limit order guarantees.

**2.** EUR/USD is at 1.0880. You want to enter long only if price breaks above 1.0920.
What order type and price?

**3.** Explain the difference between a buy limit and a buy stop, including where each is
placed relative to the current price.

**4.** Why is a stop-limit order a poor choice for a stop loss?

**5.** You are long EUR/USD with a stop at 1.0850. The mid price is 1.08560 and the spread
widens from 1.2 pips to 9 pips. Is your stop triggered? Show the bid.

**6.** Why do stop losses experience more slippage than take profit orders?

**7.** Your measured average stop slippage is 3 pips and your stop distance is 25 pips. By
what percentage does this increase your actual risk per trade?

**8.** Name the one circumstance in which moving a stop loss is acceptable, and the
direction it may move.

**9.** A trade that lost money while following your plan exactly, and a trade that made
money after you widened the stop. Which is the better trade, and why?

**10.** List five questions that must be answered before placing any trade.
