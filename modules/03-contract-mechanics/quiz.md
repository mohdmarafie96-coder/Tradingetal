# Module 03 Quiz

Closed book, calculator permitted. USD account unless stated.
[Answers](../../reference/quiz-answers.md#module-03).

**1.** How many units is 0.35 lots of a standard FX pair?

**2.** Calculate the notional value of 0.8 lots of GBP/USD at 1.2740.

**3.** What is the pip value, in USD, of 0.45 lots of EUR/USD?

**4.** What is the pip value, in USD, of 1.00 lot of USD/JPY at 152.00?

**5.** Gold has a contract size of 100 ounces. What is the value of a $1.00 move on 0.12
lots?

**6.** Long 0.6 lots EUR/USD at 1.08450, closed at 1.08790. Calculate gross P&L.

**7.** Short 0.25 lots GBP/USD at 1.26900, closed at 1.27310. Calculate gross P&L and
state whether it is a profit or loss.

**8.** You lose 35% of your account. What percentage gain is required to return to
break-even?

**9.** Long 4 CFDs of US 500 at 5,150, benchmark rate 5.0%, markup 2.5%, 365-day count.
What is one night's financing?

**10.** Explain triple swap: what it is, which night it usually falls on for FX, and why.

**11.** Account A: 1.2 pip spread, no commission. Account B: 0.2 pip spread, $3.50 per lot
per side. At 0.5 lots, which is cheaper per round trip, and by how much?

**12.** Your risk per trade is $25. A proposed trade has a round-trip cost of $9.50. State
the cost ratio and what it implies about the trade.
