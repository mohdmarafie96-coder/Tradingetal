# Module 07 Quiz

Closed book. [Answers](../../reference/quiz-answers.md#module-07).

**1.** What four values does a candlestick display?

**2.** Why are H4 and D1 timeframes recommended for beginners over M5? Give two reasons
relating to cost and one relating to psychology.

**3.** Define an uptrend in terms of swing highs and lows. What specific event ends it by
definition?

**4.** State three rules for drawing a defensible support or resistance level.

**5.** An indicator can reveal information not present in the price series. True or false?
Explain.

**6.** RSI on your instrument reads 78. A trader takes this as a sell signal. Explain what
is wrong with that reasoning.

**7.** ATR(14) on your instrument is 60 pips. Your account is $8,000 and you risk 1%. Using
a 2 ATR stop, calculate the position size in lots.

**8.** Why do moving average crossover systems perform poorly in ranging markets?

**9.** A central bank raises rates by 0.25% and the currency falls. Give the most likely
explanation.

**10.** You are long a share CFD. Earnings are announced tomorrow after the close. State
the specific risk and two actions that would address it.
