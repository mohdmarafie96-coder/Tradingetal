# Module 07 — Market Analysis

**Time:** ~5 hours · **Prerequisites:** Modules 01–06

How to read a market. This module is deliberately sceptical: analysis is where the trading
industry sells the most and delivers the least, and the honest position is that no method
of analysis predicts price reliably.

What analysis can do is give you a **repeatable, testable framework** for deciding when to
act and where to place a stop. That is genuinely useful, and it is a much more modest claim
than the one usually made.

## Lessons

1. [Reading a chart](01-reading-charts.md) — candlesticks, timeframes, what a chart is
2. [Market structure](02-market-structure.md) — trend, ranges, support and resistance
3. [Indicators](03-indicators.md) — what they do, what they cannot do
4. [Fundamentals and the calendar](04-fundamentals.md) — drivers, news, sentiment

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this module you can:

- Read a candlestick chart and describe what each candle represents
- Choose a timeframe consistent with your holding period and stop distance
- Identify trend and range conditions using swing highs and lows
- Draw support and resistance levels that are defensible rather than decorative
- Use moving averages, RSI and ATR correctly, and state the limitations of each
- Explain lag, repainting and over-fitting, and why more indicators make things worse
- Use an economic calendar to avoid trading into scheduled volatility

## A caution before you begin

Technical analysis is easy to learn badly. Patterns are visible in random data, indicators
can be tuned until any historical period looks profitable, and the feeling of
understanding a chart is almost entirely disconnected from the ability to profit from it.

Treat everything here as a **hypothesis-generating framework** that must be tested on your
own data before you rely on it. Module 08 covers how to test.
