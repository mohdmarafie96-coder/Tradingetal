# Module 06 Quiz

Closed book, calculator permitted. [Answers](../../reference/quiz-answers.md#module-06).

**1.** State the position sizing formula and explain why the position size must be
calculated last rather than chosen first.

**2.** $6,000 account, 1% risk, EUR/USD entry 1.0930, stop 1.0888. Calculate the position
size in lots, rounded down, and the actual risk achieved.

**3.** Define R and explain why journaling in R is more useful than journaling in currency.

**4.** Calculate the break-even win rate for a 2.5:1 reward-to-risk ratio.

**5.** A strategy wins 72% of the time with an average win of 0.35R. Calculate expectancy.
Is it viable? What if costs are 0.06R per trade?

**6.** Explain why a 90% win rate tells you nothing about whether a strategy is profitable.

**7.** Your account falls from a peak of $14,000 to $9,800. Calculate drawdown and the gain
required to recover.

**8.** At a 45% win rate, roughly what is the probability of at least five consecutive
losses in any 100 trades? What should a trader conclude from such a streak?

**9.** You risk 5% per trade and lose ten in a row. What percentage of your account
remains, and what gain is needed to recover?

**10.** Why should position size be reduced, not increased, during a drawdown?

**11.** You hold long EUR/USD, long GBP/USD and short USD/CHF, each at 1% risk. What is
your true exposure to a dollar rally, and why?

**12.** Explain why correlations rising during a crisis is the opposite of what a
diversified portfolio needs, and state one practical response.
