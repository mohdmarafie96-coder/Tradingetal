# Module 04 — Leverage and Margin

**Time:** ~5 hours · **Prerequisites:** Module 03

Leverage is the property that makes CFDs what they are, and margin is the accounting
system that governs it. Together they explain why a trader can be right about direction
and still lose everything.

If Module 03 was the arithmetic of a trade, this is the arithmetic of survival.

## Lessons

1. [What leverage actually does](01-leverage.md)
2. [Margin, equity and margin level](02-margin-mechanics.md)
3. [Margin call and stop-out](03-margin-call.md)
4. [Gap risk and negative balance protection](04-gap-risk.md)

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this module you can:

- Calculate leverage and margin requirement for any position
- Distinguish balance, equity, used margin, free margin and margin level, and compute each
- Predict the exact adverse price move that triggers a margin call and a stop-out
- Explain why leverage does not change your risk if position size is fixed by risk budget
- Describe how a weekend gap can bypass a stop loss entirely
- Explain what negative balance protection does and does not cover

## Key formulas

```
Margin required = notional / leverage       (= notional × margin %)
Equity          = balance + unrealised P&L
Free margin     = equity − used margin
Margin level    = (equity / used margin) × 100%
```

## The one sentence to take away

> **Leverage is not a risk setting. Position size is the risk setting.** Leverage only
> determines how much capital is reserved against a position you have already chosen.
