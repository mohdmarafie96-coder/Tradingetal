# Module 04 Quiz

Closed book, calculator permitted. [Answers](../../reference/quiz-answers.md#module-04).

**1.** State the formula relating leverage and margin percentage. What margin percentage
corresponds to 20:1?

**2.** At 30:1, what percentage adverse move eliminates the margin posted on a fully
leveraged position?

**3.** Two traders hold identical 0.2-lot positions with identical 30-pip stops. One has a
30:1 account, the other 10:1. Who loses more if stopped out? Explain.

**4.** Define equity, used margin, free margin and margin level.

**5.** Why is balance a misleading number to watch while positions are open?

**6.** At what margin level must a regulated broker begin closing retail positions, and
what is that level measured against?

**7.** $6,000 account, long 1.2 lots EUR/USD at 1.0900 at 30:1. Calculate used margin and
margin level.

**8.** For the position in question 7, approximately how many pips of adverse movement
trigger the 50% close-out? What is the loss as a percentage of the account?

**9.** Define effective leverage and state a reasonable maximum for a learning trader.

**10.** Explain why a stop loss does not guarantee your exit price.

**11.** What is negative balance protection, and what is the one thing it does *not*
protect you from?

**12.** You are long a share CFD with a stop 5% below entry. The company reports earnings
tonight. Describe the risk in terms of what the stop can and cannot do, and state two
actions that would address it.

---

**Scoring:** below 10/12, reread. Module 06 builds directly on this.
