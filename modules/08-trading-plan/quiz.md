# Module 08 Quiz

Closed book. Tick one answer per question unless the question says otherwise.

[Answers](../../reference/quiz-answers.md#module-08) unlock once you have submitted the
quiz.

**1.** Which definition of an edge is correct, and which part of it eliminates most
candidate strategies?

- [ ] a) A repeatable reason to expect positive expectancy after costs; "after costs" eliminates most candidates
- [ ] b) An indicator setting others do not use; "others do not use" is the hard part
- [ ] c) A signal that predicts direction more often than not; "predicts" is the hard part
- [ ] d) A win rate above 50%; "above 50%" is the hard part

**2.** Select the realistic sources of edge available to a retail trader. (select all that
apply)

- [ ] a) Patience and selectivity
- [ ] b) Risk management
- [ ] c) Faster access to market information than institutions
- [ ] d) Timeframe flexibility — no benchmark, no clients, no obligation to be invested
- [ ] e) Better indicator settings than other traders

**3.** Why is "buy when the trend is up and price pulls back to support" not a strategy?

- [ ] a) Because it only works on daily charts, not intraday ones
- [ ] b) Because its terms are undefined, so two people place different trades
- [ ] c) Because it omits a profit target and an exit rule entirely
- [ ] d) Because trading pullbacks does not work in any reliable way

**4.** What test determines whether your strategy is specified precisely enough?

- [ ] a) Whether the rules can be coded in under a hundred lines
- [ ] b) Whether it produces at least ten trades in an average month
- [ ] c) Whether another person following your rules places the same trades
- [ ] d) Whether the whole plan fits onto a single printed page

**5.** Select the components that must appear in a written trading plan. (select all that
apply)

- [ ] a) Risk limits with physical enforcement mechanisms
- [ ] b) Amendment rules governing when the plan may be changed
- [ ] c) Stop loss rules, including the rule for moving them
- [ ] d) A monthly profit target in currency
- [ ] e) Position sizing rule
- [ ] f) Setup definitions precise enough to replicate

**6.** Why should a trading plan include rules about when it may be amended?

- [ ] a) To make the plan longer and more thorough for later review
- [ ] b) To satisfy the record-keeping requirements of the regulator
- [ ] c) Because brokers require a versioned plan before funding
- [ ] d) Because destructive changes are made within a day of a painful loss

**7.** Why must the reasoning field in a trade journal be completed before the outcome is
known?

- [ ] a) Because reasoning written afterwards is contaminated by the result
- [ ] b) To save time when the trade is reviewed at the end of the week
- [ ] c) Because platforms overwrite the entry note once the trade closes
- [ ] d) It does not matter when it is written, only that it is written

**8.** Your journal shows an average loss of −1.3R. Select the possible explanations.
(select all that apply)

- [ ] a) Gaps taking you past the stop
- [ ] b) Exiting worse than the planned level
- [ ] c) Stops being widened or not honoured
- [ ] d) Slippage on stops
- [ ] e) Your win rate is too low

**9.** Which is a concrete example of look-ahead bias?

- [ ] a) Testing only the instruments that still exist in the market today
- [ ] b) Entering on a candle's close but recording the fill at its open
- [ ] c) Omitting commission and financing from the backtest results
- [ ] d) Adjusting a parameter repeatedly until performance improves

**10.** Your backtest shows positive expectancy but your forward test is negative. What is
the most likely explanation, and what should you do?

- [ ] a) Market conditions changed; re-optimise the parameters and retest
- [ ] b) Live markets are simply harder; fund it at a reduced size
- [ ] c) The backtest was flawed; find the specific error and do not fund it
- [ ] d) The forward sample is too small to matter; continue as planned
