# Module 08 Quiz

Closed book. [Answers](../../reference/quiz-answers.md#module-08).

**1.** Define an edge. Which part of the definition eliminates most candidate strategies?

**2.** Name three realistic sources of edge available to a retail trader, and explain why
none of them involves better analysis.

**3.** Why is "buy when the trend is up and price pulls back to support" not a strategy?

**4.** What test determines whether your strategy is specified precisely enough?

**5.** Name five components that must appear in a written trading plan.

**6.** Why should a trading plan include rules about when it may be amended?

**7.** Why must the reasoning field in a trade journal be completed before the outcome is
known?

**8.** Your journal shows an average loss of −1.3R. What are the possible explanations?

**9.** Define look-ahead bias and give a concrete example.

**10.** Your backtest shows positive expectancy but your forward test is negative. What is
the most likely explanation, and what should you do?
