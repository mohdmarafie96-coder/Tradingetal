# Module 10 Quiz

Closed book. [Answers](../../reference/quiz-answers.md#module-10).

**1.** Rank these in order of importance when choosing a broker: platform features, total
cost, regulation, spread. Justify the top item.

**2.** What is entity switching, and why does it matter for the protections you receive?

**3.** Why is comparing brokers by advertised spread misleading? Name three other costs.

**4.** What is the best practical test of a broker, and how do you perform it cheaply?

**5.** Name six items you should extract from a contract specification before trading a
new instrument.

**6.** Why should one-click trading be disabled?

**7.** When reconciling your journal against your platform statement, what is the most
common source of discrepancy?

**8.** A firm offers a 50% deposit bonus. What does this tell you about its regulatory
status, and what is the usual catch?

**9.** Explain the economic argument against buying trading signals.

**10.** Someone you met online introduces you to a trading platform showing strong returns,
and a small withdrawal succeeds. What is happening, and what should you do?
