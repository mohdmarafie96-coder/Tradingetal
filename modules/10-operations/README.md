# Module 10 — Operations

**Time:** ~4 hours · **Prerequisites:** Modules 02, 03

The practical business of trading: choosing a broker, reading a contract specification,
keeping records, and recognising the industry's failure modes before they cost you.

Unglamorous and directly financial. A bad broker choice can cost more than a bad strategy.

## Lessons

1. [Choosing a broker](01-choosing-a-broker.md)
2. [Platform and contract specifications](02-platform-and-specs.md)
3. [Records, costs and tax](03-records-and-tax.md)
4. [Scams and red flags](04-scams.md)

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this module you can:

- Verify a broker's licence on a regulator's register and identify the legal entity you
  would contract with
- Compare brokers on total cost rather than headline spread
- Read a contract specification and extract every number you need
- Keep records sufficient for tax and for your own analysis
- Recognise the common categories of trading scam and the specific signals of each
