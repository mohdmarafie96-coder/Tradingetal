# Module 09 — Trading Psychology

**Time:** ~3 hours · **Prerequisites:** Modules 06, 08

You can know everything in Modules 1 to 8 and still lose money, because knowing what to do
and doing it under pressure are different skills.

This module is not about willpower or mindset. It is about the specific, documented ways
human judgement fails under uncertainty and financial pressure, and the structural
countermeasures that work better than trying harder.

## Lessons

1. [Cognitive biases](01-biases.md) — how judgement fails, predictably
2. [Emotional states and tilt](02-tilt.md) — recognising and interrupting the spiral
3. [Discipline as a system](03-discipline.md) — structure beats willpower

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this module you can:

- Name the biases most damaging to traders and recognise them in your own journal
- Explain the disposition effect and why it destroys expectancy
- Identify your personal tilt triggers and early warning signs
- Design structural countermeasures rather than relying on resolve
- Explain why the trader who "just needs more discipline" usually needs better systems

## The core claim

> **Willpower is a poor risk control. Structure is a good one.**
>
> Do not build a plan that requires you to be disciplined at your worst moment. Build one
> that makes the bad decision difficult to execute.
