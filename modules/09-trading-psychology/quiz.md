# Module 09 Quiz

Closed book. [Answers](../../reference/quiz-answers.md#module-09).

**1.** Define the disposition effect and explain why it is fatal to expectancy.

**2.** Why is outcome bias particularly dangerous in trading, given that markets are noisy?

**3.** Describe the tilt spiral. At which step is intervention realistically possible, and
why not later?

**4.** You feel reluctant to take a setup that meets all your criteria. How do you
distinguish fear from prudence, and what does fear usually indicate?

**5.** Why is boredom a significant risk factor, and what is the structural response?

**6.** "I need to make that money back." Explain precisely what is wrong with this as a
goal.

**7.** Give three examples of replacing a willpower-based control with a structural one.

**8.** Name four conditions under which you should stop trading entirely.
