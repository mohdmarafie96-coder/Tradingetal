# Module 01 — Market Foundations

**Time:** ~3 hours · **Prerequisites:** Module 00

Before you can understand a derivative of a market, you need to understand the market.
This module covers how prices form, what the two numbers in a quote mean, who is on the
other side of your trade, and what the various asset classes actually are.

## Lessons

1. [How prices form](01-how-prices-form.md) — order books, matching, and why price moves
2. [Bid, ask and spread](02-bid-ask-spread.md) — the two-sided quote and what it costs you
3. [Liquidity, volatility and participants](03-liquidity-and-participants.md)
4. [Asset classes](04-asset-classes.md) — FX, indices, commodities, shares, crypto, bonds

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this module you can:

- Explain how a limit order book matches buyers and sellers, and what "the price" means
- Correctly identify which side of a quote you transact on when buying and when selling
- Calculate a spread in absolute terms and as a cost on a given position size
- Explain why spreads widen, and name three predictable times they do
- Distinguish liquidity from volatility and describe how each affects your execution
- Describe the main CFD asset classes and their characteristic behaviours
