# Module 01 Quiz

Closed book. Tick one answer per question unless the question says otherwise.

[Answers](../../reference/quiz-answers.md#module-01) unlock once you have submitted the
quiz.

**1.** EUR/USD is quoted 1.09140 / 1.09155. You open a long position. What price do you
get, and what is the spread in pips?

- [ ] a) 1.091475, spread 1.5 pips
- [ ] b) 1.09155, spread 1.5 pips
- [ ] c) 1.09140, spread 1.5 pips
- [ ] d) 1.09155, spread 15 pips

**2.** A market order differs from a limit order in that it:

- [ ] a) Controls price but not execution
- [ ] b) Adds liquidity to the order book
- [ ] c) Controls execution but not price
- [ ] d) Controls both

**3.** Can price move with no transactions occurring at all?

- [ ] a) No — price only changes when a trade executes
- [ ] b) Only when the broker manually adjusts its quote
- [ ] c) Yes, but only on exchange-traded instruments, never in FX
- [ ] d) Yes — if every seller below a level cancels, the best ask jumps on zero volume

**4.** You are long 1 standard lot of EUR/USD at a 1.2 pip spread. Immediately after your
fill, before any market movement, what is your unrealised P&L?

- [ ] a) −$12.00
- [ ] b) −$1.20
- [ ] c) $0.00
- [ ] d) −$120.00

**5.** Which period normally offers the tightest FX spreads?

- [ ] a) The hour after the New York close
- [ ] b) London/New York overlap
- [ ] c) Sydney session only
- [ ] d) Tokyo session only

**6.** Are liquidity and volatility the same thing?

- [ ] a) Yes — both measure how much price moves
- [ ] b) They are different names for the spread
- [ ] c) No — an exotic pair in a political crisis has high volatility and low liquidity
- [ ] d) No — but high liquidity always implies high volatility

**7.** You hold a share CFD overnight. The company reports bad earnings and the stock
opens 12% below your stop loss level. What price are you filled at?

- [ ] a) The order is cancelled and the position stays open
- [ ] b) At the previous close, since the gap is ignored
- [ ] c) At your stop price — that is what a stop guarantees
- [ ] d) At the first available price after the gap, roughly 12% below the previous close

**8.** Why do most index CFDs sometimes diverge from the cash index quoted on the news?

- [ ] a) They are priced off the index future, which carries cost of carry and no dividends
- [ ] b) A currency conversion is applied to the quoted index level
- [ ] c) The broker adds a random markup to the cash index level
- [ ] d) News outlets report the previous day's close rather than the live level

**9.** A broker operating a B-book:

- [ ] a) Charges no spread
- [ ] b) Internalises your position, so your loss is its gain
- [ ] c) Is acting illegally
- [ ] d) Always hedges your position in the underlying market

**10.** EUR/USD is quoted 1.08472. It reaches 1.08477 and you think price has moved 5
pips. What have you actually measured?

- [ ] a) 5 pips — the measurement is correct
- [ ] b) 0.05 pips
- [ ] c) 5 pipettes, which is 0.5 pips
- [ ] d) 50 pips
