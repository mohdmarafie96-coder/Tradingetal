# Module 01 Quiz

Closed book. [Answers](../../reference/quiz-answers.md#module-01).

**1.** EUR/USD is quoted 1.09140 / 1.09155. You want to open a long position. What price
do you get, and what is the spread in pips?

**2.** A market order differs from a limit order in that it:

- a) Controls price but not execution
- b) Controls execution but not price
- c) Controls both
- d) Adds liquidity to the order book

**3.** Price can move with no transactions occurring at all. True or false, and why?

**4.** You are long 1 standard lot of EUR/USD. The spread is 1.2 pips. Immediately after
your fill, before any market movement, what is your unrealised P&L in dollars?

**5.** Which period normally offers the tightest FX spreads?

- a) Sydney session only
- b) Tokyo session only
- c) London/New York overlap
- d) The hour after the New York close

**6.** Liquidity and volatility are the same thing. True or false? Give an example that
supports your answer.

**7.** You hold a share CFD overnight and the company reports bad earnings before the
open. The stock opens 12% below your stop loss level. What price do you get filled at?

**8.** Why do most index CFDs sometimes diverge from the cash index quoted on the news?

**9.** A broker operating a B-book:

- a) Always hedges your position in the underlying market
- b) Internalises your position, so your loss is its gain
- c) Is acting illegally
- d) Charges no spread

**10.** You see EUR/USD quoted as 1.08472 and think price has moved 5 "pips" when it
reaches 1.08477. What have you actually measured, and what is the risk of this mistake?

---

**Scoring:** below 8/10, reread before continuing. Modules 3 and 4 assume all of this.
