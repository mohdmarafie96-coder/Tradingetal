# Module 00 Quiz

Closed book. [Answers](../../reference/quiz-answers.md#module-00).

**1.** Approximately what percentage of retail CFD accounts lose money, according to
regulator-mandated broker disclosures?

- a) 20–30%
- b) 40–50%
- c) 70–85%
- d) 95–99%

**2.** Which of these is charged on a position regardless of whether it is profitable?

- a) The spread only
- b) Overnight financing only
- c) Spread, commission and overnight financing
- d) None; costs are netted against profit at withdrawal

**3.** Can a US retail client open a CFD account with a regulated broker?

- a) Yes, with any broker
- b) Yes, but only with US-regulated brokers
- c) No; CFDs are not permitted for US retail clients
- d) Only with an account balance above $25,000

**4.** Why should you set your demo account balance to a realistic figure rather than
accepting the $100,000 default?

- a) Large demo balances are charged higher simulated commission
- b) Position sizes that feel reasonable on a large balance are ruinous on a small one
- c) Brokers restrict features on high-balance demos
- d) It affects the spread you are quoted

**5.** Which of the following does a demo account simulate *poorly*?

- a) Order types and order management
- b) Margin and P&L arithmetic
- c) Execution quality and emotional pressure
- d) Contract specifications

**6.** Your stated reason for trading is "to replace my salary within six months". This
is a warning sign primarily because:

- a) Six months is too short to learn the platform
- b) Capital under pressure to perform produces worse decisions, and the timeframe
  presumes an outcome the base rates do not support
- c) Brokers do not permit full-time trading on retail accounts
- d) Tax treatment differs for full-time traders
