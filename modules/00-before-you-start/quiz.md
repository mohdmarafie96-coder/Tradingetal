**1.** Approximately what percentage of retail CFD accounts lose money, according to
regulator-mandated broker disclosures?

- [ ] a) 20–30%
- [ ] b) 40–50%

- [ ] d) 95–99%

**2.** Which of these is charged on a position regardless of whether it is profitable?

- [ ] a) Spread, commission and overnight financing
- [ ] b) None; costs are netted against profit at withdrawal
- [ ] c) The spread only
- [ ] d) Overnight financing only

**3.** Can a US retail client open a CFD account with a regulated broker?

- [ ] a) Yes, but only with US-regulated brokers
- [ ] b) No; CFDs are not permitted for US retail clients

accepting the $100,000 default?

- [ ] a) It affects the spread you are quoted
- [ ] b) Large demo balances are charged higher simulated commission
- [ ] c) Position sizes that feel reasonable on a large balance are ruinous on a small one
- [ ] d) Brokers restrict features on high-balance demos

**5.** Which of the following does a demo account simulate *poorly*?

- [ ] a) Order types and order management
- [ ] b) Contract specifications
- [ ] c) Margin and P&L arithmetic
- [ ] d) Execution quality and emotional pressure

**6.** Your stated reason for trading is "to replace my salary within six months". This is
a warning sign primarily because:

- [ ] a) Capital under pressure to perform produces worse decisions, and the base rates do not support it
- [ ] b) Full-time traders are taxed differently, which changes the return needed
- [ ] c) Brokers do not permit retail clients to trade as their sole occupation
