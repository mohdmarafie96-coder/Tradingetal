# Module 02 Quiz

Closed book. [Answers](../../reference/quiz-answers.md#module-02).

**1.** Define a CFD in one sentence, naming the counterparty.

**2.** You hold 1,000 share CFDs in a company that goes into administration. What is your
claim on the company's assets?

**3.** Why do CFDs incur overnight financing when shares do not?

**4.** Under UK/EU retail rules, what leverage is available on a major FX pair, and what
margin percentage does that imply?

**5.** At what level of equity relative to required margin must a broker begin closing
your positions?

- a) 100%
- b) 75%
- c) 50%
- d) 25%

**6.** Explain negative balance protection and name the historical event that most
clearly demonstrated why it is needed.

**7.** A broker offers you professional client status with higher leverage. Name three
protections you would lose.

**8.** You are short 200 share CFDs over an ex-dividend date. The gross dividend is $0.80
and the net is $0.68. What is the adjustment to your account, and in which direction?

**9.** Long positions have bounded maximum loss and short positions do not. Explain why,
and explain what practically limits the short loss in a regulated retail account.

**10.** A firm advertises a 100% deposit bonus and 400:1 leverage. What does this tell you
about its regulatory status, and what is the most common way clients discover the problem?

**Scoring:** below 8/10, reread before continuing.
