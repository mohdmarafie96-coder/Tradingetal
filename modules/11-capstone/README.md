# Module 11 — Capstone

**Time:** 8+ weeks · **Prerequisites:** all previous modules

Everything up to here was preparation. This module is the work.

You will write a complete trading plan, backtest it, run an eight-week forward test on
demo, measure the results honestly, and make a decision about whether to continue.

## Contents

1. [The eight-week programme](01-programme.md) — week by week
2. [Final assessment](02-assessment.md) — can you do the arithmetic?
3. [The decision](03-decision.md) — go live, keep testing, or stop

## Deliverables

By the end you will have produced:

- [ ] A written trading plan, versioned and dated
- [ ] A backtest of at least 100 trades with full metrics
- [ ] A forward test of at least 40 trades over eight weeks
- [ ] A complete trade journal with process-compliance tracking
- [ ] Eight weekly reviews and two monthly reviews
- [ ] A written decision with reasons

## The honest framing

The most likely outcomes of this programme, roughly in order of probability:

1. **Your strategy does not have positive expectancy after costs.** Most do not. You will
   have learned this for free.
2. **You cannot follow your own rules yet.** Compliance below 90%. Common, fixable, and
   more important to fix than the strategy.
3. **The results are ambiguous.** 40 trades is a small sample. Continue testing.
4. **Positive expectancy and good compliance.** Less common. Then the question of live
   trading becomes real.

Outcome 1 is a success. Outcome 2 is a success. The failure mode is not any of these; it is
funding an account without having found out which one you are in.
