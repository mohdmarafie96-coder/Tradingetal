# Pre-Trade Checklist

Complete this **in full** before every trade. Physically. Ninety seconds.

If any box cannot be ticked, **the trade is not taken.**

---

**Date / time:** ___________  **Instrument:** ___________  **Direction:** ___________

## Setup

- [ ] A defined setup from my plan is present — which one? ___________
- [ ] Every condition of that setup is satisfied
- [ ] No disqualifying condition is present
- [ ] The higher timeframe direction agrees
- [ ] I can state my thesis in one sentence: ______________________________________

## Levels

- [ ] Entry price / trigger: ___________
- [ ] Stop price: ___________ — justified by: ___________
- [ ] Stop distance: ______ pips/points
- [ ] Stop is at least ______ (my minimum, e.g. 1.5 ATR) from entry
- [ ] Target price: ___________ — justified by: ___________
- [ ] Reward-to-risk: ______ : 1 — meets my minimum of ______

## Sizing

- [ ] Account equity: ___________
- [ ] Risk budget (___% ): ___________
- [ ] Calculated position size: ___________ (**rounded down**)
- [ ] Actual risk in currency: ___________
- [ ] Actual risk as % of equity: ______%
- [ ] Size is at or above the platform minimum

## Account state

- [ ] Notional value: ___________
- [ ] Effective leverage after this trade: ______ : 1 — within my limit
- [ ] Total open risk including this trade: ______% — within my limit
- [ ] Correlation group exposure: ______% — within my limit
- [ ] Number of open positions after this: ______ — within my limit
- [ ] Margin level after this trade: ______%

## Costs

- [ ] Estimated round-trip cost: ___________
- [ ] Cost ratio (cost ÷ risk): ______% — below 25%
- [ ] Expected nights held: ______; financing cost: ___________

## Events

- [ ] Economic calendar checked
- [ ] No high-impact release within my stated window
- [ ] No earnings before my expected exit (shares)
- [ ] Weekend exposure considered
- [ ] Rollover / triple swap considered

## Limits

- [ ] I have not hit my daily loss limit
- [ ] I have not hit my weekly loss limit
- [ ] I am not currently exhibiting any tilt warning sign from my list
- [ ] I am not taking this trade to recover a previous loss

## Execution

- [ ] Order will be placed as a bracket, with stop and target attached
- [ ] Journal entry will be written immediately, before the outcome is known

---

**All boxes ticked?** If not, **do not trade.**
